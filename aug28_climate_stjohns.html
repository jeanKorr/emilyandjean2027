St. Johns West Climate Station · NL, Canada

Weather on August 28

Historical records · 2010–2025
August 28 in St. John's West is typically a mild late-summer day. Temperatures have ranged widely on this date — from a record low of 4.7°C (2017) on an unseasonably cool year to a warm high of 26.0°C (2012). The middle of that range sits around 15°C, which is fairly representative of what you'd expect on a typical late August afternoon. Precipitation is generally light on this date, with the greatest single-day total on record being 11.7 mm in 2014 — modest by Newfoundland standards.
